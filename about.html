Who We Are

Kilo Auto Spares Ltd has been keeping Nairobi's vehicles on the road with quality, durable and affordable parts.

Our Story

Kilo Auto Spares Ltd is based on Witu Road, off Lusaka Road, at Brunei House in Nairobi. We stock a wide range of spare parts for both Japanese and European vehicles, from suspension and braking systems to engine filters and electrical components.

Our goal is simple: help drivers, mechanics and garages find the right part, at a fair price, without the guesswork. Every part we sell is chosen for reliability, so you get quality that lasts — not just the cheapest option on the shelf.
